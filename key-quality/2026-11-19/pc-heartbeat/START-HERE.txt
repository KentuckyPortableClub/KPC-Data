KPC heartbeat (19 Nov 2026)

What it is: every 20 minutes your PC looks at when the club's two watchdog bots last ran on GitHub.
If one is overdue (GitHub skipped it, as on the morning of 19 Nov), the PC starts it once.
The watchdog then restarts whatever timed bot was missed. It starts nothing else and changes nothing.

Setup (2 minutes), with the PC on and gh logged in (the same login your PC helper uses):
  1. Put this folder anywhere that stays put (for example next to your pc-runner folder).
  2. Double-click 1-TEST-HEARTBEAT.bat   -> you should see one line naming KPC-Links and KPC-Data with minutes.
  3. Double-click 2-HEARTBEAT-ON.bat     -> it is on. No window pops up; kpc-heartbeat.log gets one line per look.
  To stop it: 3-HEARTBEAT-OFF.bat

It only works while the PC is on and online. The watchdog bots never go red, so this sends you no emails.
The look-and-decide part is tested; the three .bat files could not be tried on Windows from the chat,
so if step 2 or 3 shows an error, send the text of it to a chat.
